Off-road coaching on heavy adventure and dual-sport motorcycles only works when the terrain, pace, and physical load match the rider and the machine. Before confirming dates, we discuss your background, your motorcycle setup, and what you actually want to achieve.

## How Booking Works

1. **Send Your Initial Details:** Use the checklist below to send a short message via email, WhatsApp, or Telegram.
2. **Direct Assessment:** I review your riding experience, bike/tire setup, and preferred dates to confirm whether the format fits—or suggest a better progression.
3. **Dates & Coaching Rate Confirmation:** Once we agree on the format (1-on-1 or your pre-formed private group of up to 5 riders), we lock in the dates.
4. **Independent Travel & Lodging:** You arrange your own travel and accommodation in Bansko (I can share vetted local recommendations upon request, with zero agency involvement or markup).

---

## What to Include in Your Message

To give you an accurate answer on availability, terrain suitability, and coaching rates without back-and-forth, include these six points:

1. **Name & Country:** Where you are traveling from and your preferred language (English, Russian, or Bulgarian).
2. **Format & Group Size:** 1-on-1 coaching or a pre-formed private group (exact number of riders, maximum 5).
3. **Motorcycle(s) & Tires:** Exact make and model, plus currently fitted tires. *(Note: minimum 50/50 dual-sport tread is required for mountain dirt roads; road-biased tires are suitable only for basic closed-ground drills.)*
4. **Riding Background:** Years on pavement, off-road experience level, and honest self-assessment of where you feel stuck or fatigued.
5. **Physical Constraints:** Any lower-back, knee, shoulder, or wrist injuries we should account for when structuring posture and session length.
6. **Preferred Format & Dates:** Approximate dates and whether you are looking at a 1–2 day training ground block, a 1–2 day mountain training ride, or a 3+ day field coaching block.
