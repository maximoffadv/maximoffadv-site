# Privacy Notice

**Maximoffadv** operates as an independent off-road motorcycle coaching practice based in Bansko, Bulgaria. This notice explains what personal information is collected when you submit an inquiry and how it is handled under EU data protection rules (GDPR).

---

## 1. Data Controller & Contact

- **Service:** Maximoffadv (Bansko, Bulgaria)
- **Contact Email:** exhilaration.zone@gmail.com

---

## 2. What Information Is Collected & Why

When you use the contact form or write directly via email or messenger, you voluntarily provide:

- **Contact details:** Name, country, preferred language, email address, and optional messenger handle (WhatsApp / Telegram).
- **Riding & motorcycle details:** Bike model, tire setup, riding experience, and preferred dates.
- **Voluntary physical notes:** Any injuries or physical limitations you choose to mention so session pace, posture drills, and terrain load can be adjusted safely.

This information is used **solely** to evaluate training suitability, check schedule availability, and reply to your inquiry. It is never sold, shared for marketing, or added to mailing lists.

---

## 3. Data Processing & Storage

- **Form delivery:** Inquiries sent through the website form are routed via **Web3Forms** directly to `exhilaration.zone@gmail.com` (Google Mail).
- **Retention:** Inquiry correspondence is kept only as long as necessary to coordinate your training or ride, after which it can be deleted upon request.
- **Cookies & tracking:** This is a static website. It does not use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party analytics profiles.

---

## 4. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under EU GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have sent. To do so, write to `exhilaration.zone@gmail.com`.
