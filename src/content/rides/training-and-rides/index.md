# Training & Riding Formats

**Off-road coaching on your own motorcycle — adapted to what your riding actually needs once we see you on the bike.**

Every rider brings a different mix of habits, tension points, physical limits, and motorcycle setup. One rider over-grips the bars; another loses balance on slow turns or overloads the lower back as soon as the surface gets rough. Because real bottlenecks only show up during practical diagnostic drills on the bike, exact training plans are never locked in blindly in advance—we shape and adjust the work on the ground based on what we observe.

> **1-on-1 or Pre-Formed Private Groups Only (Max 5 Riders)**  
> For core coaching sessions, I do not mix strangers into random groups. You book either individual instruction or bring your own pre-formed group of up to 5 friends or riding partners. Keeping training groups private and familiar keeps the pace consistent, eliminates ego competition, and ensures every rider gets direct, individual feedback.

## Core Coaching & Riding Formats

### 1. Training Ground Sessions (1–2 Days)
Closed-ground work where we start with diagnostic exercises to see how you and your motorcycle interact, then build cleaner, more repeatable control without traffic or steep drop-offs. What we prioritize depends directly on the gaps revealed during the initial drills.

### 2. Weekend Training Rides (1–2 Days)
Moving from controlled ground work onto real mountain tracks, unpaved dirt and gravel roads, and varied gradients *(minimum 50/50 dual-sport tires required)*. Short roadside observations and adjustments connect directly to what happens on live terrain as pace, surface, and fatigue change.

### 3. Multi-Day Field Coaching (3+ Days)
Extended mountain riding across the Rhodope Mountains and selected areas of Rila where consistency is tested over several days. Rather than following a rigid script, daily adjustments respond to how your technique holds up under accumulating fatigue and changing terrain.

![Riders and coaching on a mountain track in the Rhodope Mountains](./mountain-trail-coaching.jpg)
*Applying ground mechanics on live mountain routes across the Rhodopes.*

### 4. Custom Multi-Day Training Camps (5–21 Days)
Built around a pre-formed group of **2 to 5 riders** (often riders with road experience or limited off-road time looking to develop solid dirt control over a longer stay).

- **Flexible rhythm:** Combines training ground work, mountain rides, and rest days, shaped around your group and adjusted in real time as skills and fatigue evolve.
- **You pay only for active training days:** Rest and recovery days (thermal springs, SPA, maintenance, or downtime) are not billed—charges apply strictly to days with active coaching.

### 5. Route Scouting & Reconnaissance Rides *(By Invitation / Vetted Riders Only)*
Working reconnaissance rides to explore and verify new mountain routes—not commercial riding packages. Expect dead ends, fallen timber, rough ungraded sections, and tight turnarounds. Open only to vetted riders I have already trained or ridden with whose bike control, judgment, and mechanical readiness are proven.

## Bi-Weekly Open Practice Rides (5+ Riders)

Alongside our private 1-on-1 and small-group coaching, we run larger open practice rides twice a month on weekends for groups of **more than 5 riders** (each rider on their own motorcycle, handling their own travel and accommodation). Depending on the schedule and ground conditions, these take one of two formats:

- **2-Day Weekend Practice Ride:** Saturday and Sunday group riding and technique practice across mountain routes.
- **Combined Weekend Format:** 1 day of closed-ground drills + 1 day out on a mountain route.

If one of these weekend sessions falls within your 5–21 day training camp and fits your current skill level, you are welcome to join the group—or use those days for rest and recovery instead.
